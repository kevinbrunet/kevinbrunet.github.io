I am **Kévin Brunet**. I work on software architecture, information systems and AI agents.

## Architecture must work in the real world

Architecture decisions must account for the business, budget, existing systems and organization. They must remain understandable to the people who build, operate and evolve the system.

I look for solutions that are robust enough to last, but no more complex than necessary. The right choice is the one a team can explain, implement and own over time.

## Putting AI in the right place

An AI agent does not remain a simple feature for long. As soon as it accesses data, uses tools or acts for a user, it becomes part of the information system.

That raises concrete questions: Who acts? For what purpose? With which permissions? Based on what information? Who reviews the outcome? What evidence do we retain?

I focus on the transition from demonstration to real-world use. This is where identity, delegation, memory, security, observability and human oversight become essential.

## Making mechanisms visible

I write to share what I observe and make technical subjects easier to discuss. I do not try to make complexity disappear. I try to separate what is necessary from what we create ourselves, then find words and diagrams that make it understandable.

The common thread is simple: **understand how a system works so that we can trust it.**
