A rule in a prompt remains advice. A grammar can remove forbidden tokens before selection.

Through Maya, a support adviser searching for Camille Martin's record in Lyon, this series explores the separation of reasoning and decision, constraints generated from the actual schema, the cost of a usable output, IntelliSense approaches and the limitations of frontier models.
